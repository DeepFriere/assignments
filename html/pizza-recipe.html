Pepperoni Pizza Recipe

Ingredients:

Mozerella Cheese, Tomato Sauce, Garlic, Pepperoni, Pizza Dough


Instructions: 1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
3. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
4. Sprinkle a generous amount of shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
5. Arrange the pepperoni slices evenly on top of the cheese.
6. Add minced garlic for extra flavor, if desired.
7. Transfer the pizza to a baking sheet or pizza stone and bake in the pre heated oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
8. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy your homemade pepperoni pizza!
